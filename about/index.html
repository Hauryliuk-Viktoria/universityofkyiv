About University of Kyiv

Kyiv University is a modern educational institution that provides high-quality higher education. The university enjoys a well-deserved reputation as one of the leading universities not only in Ukraine but also in Europe. This is due to the high level of student training, the wide selection of relevant and in-demand specializations, and the excellent career opportunities that await graduates. Our degrees are recognized internationally, enabling graduates to build successful careers both in Ukraine and abroad.

We place special emphasis on developing academic programs that follow international standards and incorporate the best practices from global education. A key aspect of our approach is our participation in international initiatives such as the TEMPUS programs and the Visegrad Fund, allowing us to continually improve the quality of education and integrate the latest innovations into our system. We train highly qualified specialists who become recognized experts and sought-after professionals in both the Ukrainian and global job markets.
